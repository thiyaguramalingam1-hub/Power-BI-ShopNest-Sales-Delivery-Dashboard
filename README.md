# 🛒 ShopNest Retail Sales & Delivery Performance Dashboard

**Tool:** Power BI

## 📌 Situation
ShopNest, an online retail store, needed visibility into sales performance and delivery efficiency across categories, states and time periods.

## 🎯 Task
Build an interactive Power BI dashboard to track revenue, identify top categories, and pinpoint delivery delays.

## 🛠️ Action
- Designed **8 KPI visualizations**: yearly revenue trends, monthly delivery performance, state-wise and seasonal sales patterns
- Analyzed top-selling categories, delayed orders by category, payment method trends and product ratings

## 📈 Results
- Revenue grew from **₹0.1M (2016)** to **₹8.6M (2018)**
- **beleza_saude** was the top category at **₹1.45M**
- **cama_mesa_banho** had the most delivery delays: **920 orders**
